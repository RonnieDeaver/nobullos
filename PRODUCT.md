# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Growth-minded law-firm owners, managing partners, and operators — typically solo practitioners to mid-size firms — who are tired of spending on marketing that does not produce signed cases. They evaluate vendors skeptically, read results before promises, and respond to commercially serious language. They are not marketers; they want to hand the problem to someone who will own it.

## Product Purpose

NoBull Marketing is a done-for-you revenue engineering agency for law firms. It connects marketing, intake, and sales into a complete revenue system — the Revenue Engineering System — so that law firms produce more leads, more consults, and more signed cases in a measurable, repeatable way.

Primary conversion: **Book a Strategy Call** (currently labelled "Book a Free Demo" via Calendly).

## Positioning

The only legal marketing agency that engineers the full revenue path — marketing, intake, and sales as one integrated system — rather than handing the firm a stream of leads and stopping. NoBull has codified this system in a published book (*Revenue Engineering for Law Firms*) and applied it across hundreds of law firms.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from Google search, referral, and content; they want to verify credibility before booking.
- The demo/strategy-call booking lives in Calendly (inline embed); the firm must not lose that embed.
- A contact form handles non-booking inquiries.
- Resources (articles, videos, webinars, podcasts) support organic search and credibility.
- The Revenue Engineering book (*Revenue Engineering for Law Firms* by Ronnie Deaver) is a published IP asset sold on Amazon and Audible.
- DNS for nobullmarketing.com will be pointed to this Replit deployment; until then the site is accessible at `/website-preview/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site generated by `website/generate.ts` → `website/public/`; no SSR per page.
- All internal links are relative (works at domain root and at `/website-preview/`).
- The marketing bundle is served only on `nobullmarketing.com` and `www.nobullmarketing.com`; all other hosts serve the NoBull OS app.
- `reports.nobullmarketing.com` is a fixed OAuth callback host for Zoom and Front; it must never serve the marketing site.
- CSP for marketing pages is managed separately from the OS app's helmet CSP.
- 16 existing article URLs (`/resource/<slug>/`) carry SEO equity and must not break.
- All factual claims must come exclusively from `docs/CONTENT_TRUTH_SOURCE.md`.

## Product Principles

1. **The revenue system is the product.** Marketing, intake, and sales are one engineered whole, not three optional services. Every visual and copy decision must reinforce integration, not commodity services.
2. **Credibility before enthusiasm.** Law-firm owners are skeptical buyers. Proof — named clients, specific results, published book, team bios — earns the booking. Excitement without evidence is noise.
3. **Own the full path to commitment.** The site's job ends at a booked strategy call, not a click. Every section removes a barrier or reinforces the decision to book.
4. **Restraint is the premium signal.** Typography, proportion, and material realism carry authority. Decoration, gradients, and generic SaaS chrome undermine it.
5. **Preserve what works.** Sixteen resource URLs, Calendly integration, contact form, Vimeo embeds, and the `www → apex` redirect carry existing equity. Nothing ships that breaks them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
