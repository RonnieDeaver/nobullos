# Creative Direction
## Merge the Existing NoBull Brand With The Law Firm Revenue Engine

This is not a replacement of NoBull Marketing. It is a controlled extension of the established brand so it can house a more premium flagship property: **The Law Firm Revenue Engine**.

## Brand balance

- Approximately **70–75% existing NoBull**
- Approximately **25–30% book-inspired Revenue Engine**
- NoBull Marketing remains the parent brand.
- The Revenue Engine is the flagship intellectual property.

## Parent brand

NoBull should continue to communicate:

- Trustworthy
- Established
- Intelligent
- Approachable
- Competent

Use:

- Eggshell and warm-white surfaces
- Crimson Pro editorial typography
- Sweet Sans Pro supporting typography, only when licensed files exist
- Crimson calls to action
- Earth body copy
- Liberty Blue headings or supporting panels
- Goldenrod used sparingly
- Pinstripes, one-pixel outlines, clean framing, and restrained data visualization

## Revenue Engine layer

The Revenue Engine adds:

- Ambition
- Engineering
- Authority
- High performance
- Commercial seriousness

Use this layer primarily in:

- The contained mechanical area behind or beside the book in the hero
- One deliberate full-width dark system section
- Small controlled references near the closing CTA

Within that layer, use:

- Charcoal or near-black
- White typography
- Controlled metallic gold
- Machinery and engineered components
- Schematic lines
- Subtle sparks and dimensional lighting
- CaseGen → CaseIntake → CaseConvert → Signed Cases

## Exact assets

Use the exact supplied assets. Never redraw, recolor, crop, distort, rearrange, or approximate them.

- Full-color NoBull logo on light backgrounds
- Reverse NoBull logo on Crimson or dark backgrounds
- Exact book cover as supplied
- Create book perspective, page edges, spine, and shadow around the exact cover rather than regenerating it

## Homepage structure

1. Light NoBull header
2. Light NoBull-led hero with the exact book on the right
3. One concentrated dark Revenue Engine system band
4. Return to Eggshell for metrics and proof
5. Traditional NoBull services and case studies
6. Verified client proof
7. Light closing CTA connecting back to the Revenue Engine
8. Crimson NoBull footer

## Do not

- Make the full website black and gold
- Replace the NoBull logo with a generic gold bull
- Recreate the logo or book cover
- Introduce a completely new luxury identity
- Use machinery behind every section
- Repeat the torn-currency treatment outside the cover
- Let the book typography replace NoBull typography
- Publish fabricated metrics, ratings, testimonials, law-firm logos, case-study outcomes, awards, or trademark symbols
- Use generic SaaS cards, glassmorphism, purple gradients, pill saturation, or random glows

The intended result is an established law-firm growth company unveiling its most powerful proprietary system—not an old company being erased by a new identity.
