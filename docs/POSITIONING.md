## One-Liner

The site's operative one-liner is the locked H1 pair: *"More Leads Aren't Enough. We Turn Them
Into Signed Cases."* — problem + outcome in 10 words, concrete noun ("signed cases"), contrarian
against the industry's more-leads default. Assessed, not rewritten (locked).
