# Website Messaging Architecture — The Law Firm Revenue Engine™

Sitewide messaging contract for nobullmarketing.com, derived from the
copy brief (2026-08), the Revenue Engine sales deck (July 13, 2026), and
the book manuscript *The Law Firm Revenue Engine*. Source priority when
they conflict: **brief > deck > book > existing site copy**.

Companion docs: `website-claim-ledger.md` (every number),
`website-copy-audit.md` (route-by-route findings),
`website-final-copy.md` (decided copy), `website-copy-changelog.md`.

## 1. Core positioning

**One sentence:** NoBull Marketing doesn't stop at leads — we engineer
the entire path from first click to signed case.

**The promise (book final editorial review, 2026-08-08 — Task #4120):**
more top-line revenue from the same leads. Revenue language leads
everywhere; "growth" survives only inside factual scale claims (350+
law firms grown, locations added — kept unhedged) and the locked hero
eyebrow (revenue rewording proposed, changelog Task #4120 owner flags),
never as the stated goal.

**Hierarchy (locked):** Money → Revenue Engine → REE → Tuning. Revenue
is the goal; REE is the score that tells you how expensive that revenue
is to produce; the systems are the levers you tune. No surface may
invert this — REE is never presented as the goal itself.

**The argument, in order:**
1. More options, more proof, less patience: prospects compare before
   calling and choose the easiest path (deck S4).
2. The same lead flow is worth far more in a firm that captures and
   converts it well (deck S5–S6). Leads aren't the constraint — the
   leaks between lead, consult, and client are.
3. Three core functions drive revenue: Marketing brings in leads. Intake
   books consults. Sales signs clients. Revenue stalls when one is weak;
   it compounds when all three are strong (deck S12).
4. The Law Firm Revenue Engine™ connects all three into one measured
   system — CaseGen™, CaseIntake™, CaseConvert™ — tracked click-to-close.
5. REE (Revenue Engine Efficiency = attributable top-line revenue ÷
   Growth Investment) is the single public score, and it stands on its
   own — never introduced by comparison to other metrics (the
   ad-spend-ratio contrast was deleted sitewide 2026-08-09, Task #4166 —
   lawyers don't think in ad-spend ratios; the acronym itself is banned
   from copy and these docs by lint-website-banned-phrases, and the
   copy-changelog keeps the verbatim owner rationale). /calculator/
   owns the sitewide introduction AND the definition since Task #4987
   REMOVED the homepage REE strip wholesale (owner decision: zero REE
   mentions on the homepage; the strip had owned the definition from
   Task #4120 — scoreboard form, Task #4166; Task #4259 added the
   gap→score connection line, the WHAT MOVES THE SCORE four-lever
   list, and the diagnostic-role clarifier — the score is the
   diagnostic, never the goal, never a fourth system). The #system
   region carries NO lever lines since Task #4295 (the owner-brief
   six-scene model dropped the #4120 per-stage lever tails — and the
   #4837 engine story keeps them out; the strip's WHAT MOVES THE SCORE
   list was the four-lever story's only rendered home until it left
   with the strip), and since Task #4924 it references the score not
   at all: the retired name-only line (`Revenue is the outcome. REE is
   the scorecard.`) left with the recap the customization band
   replaced — and since Task #4987 NO homepage surface names the score
   at all (the homepage footer's calculator link is relabeled
   `Revenue Calculator`).
6. Diagnosis beats guessing: "Guessing creates activity. Diagnosis
   creates growth." (deck S11).

## 3. Voice

- Plain-spoken, direct, specific. Short declarative sentences.
- Evidence over adjectives. Every big claim sits next to its number or
  its mechanism. No "cutting-edge", "unleash", "supercharge".
- Confident, never hype: we say what the system does and prove it. We
  never promise "effortless" — implementation is staged, tracking-first,
  and low-disruption, not magic.
- "Noble. No B.S." — honest about what we don't do (no invented stats,
  no merged numbers, no unnamed "studies").
- Diagnostic frame: leaks, constraints, tune — the reader should feel
  their firm is a machine that can be measured and fixed. ("Throttle"
  left this vocabulary on 2026-08-08: the site never frames growth as
  a dial you can turn down — owner directive 2026-08-07, see §6 #8.)
- Balance: roughly 70–75% NoBull brand voice (plain growth language),
  25–30% engine-layer naming. Pages must read naturally if the reader
  skips every ™ term.

**Book voice (Task #4032 — governed the cinematic stage copy, governs
the #4837 engine-story copy and any
future homepage copy pass):** the owner's manuscript is the voice
reference; mine lines from it near-verbatim before inventing new ones.
- Traits: warm and direct-address ("you're under the hood now"), plain
  first person where natural, short declarative chains ("Marketing
  brings in leads. Intake books consultations. Sales signs cases." —
  book-only since Task #4259: the opener body no longer renders the
  chain; the gap band's handoffs bridge owns the three-function walk),
  concrete outcomes over abstractions, idiom over ad-speak ("ringing
  off the hook", "that's not money").
- Authentic phrase bank: Intro — "Let's open the hood." (validated the
  locked OPEN THE HOOD eyebrow until Task #4837 retired it with the
  cinematic). Ch1 — "Now it's your turn to see how
  the engine turns.", "Altogether, that's a Revenue Engine.", "a phone
  rings out to a full voicemail machine. That's not money.", "A strong
  Revenue Engine spirals a business upward very quickly as success
  feeds greater success." (mined by stage 04's terminus until Task
  #4259 retired the spiral clause — the phrase stays available),
  consultations that "stalled out". Ch2 —
  "injecting fuel into an engine", "ringing off the hook, but you'll
  still need to be able to pick up", "every possible drop-out point",
  "No one buys a lawyer. No one buys your law degree. They buy help.",
  "Reviews fuel rankings. Rankings fuel leads. Leads fuel cases. Cases
  fuel more reviews. That is the flywheel." Ch4 (dials, throttle,
  growth-and-consolidation, "you have control") is BOOK-ONLY — owner
  directive 2026-08-07: never mine Ch4 for site copy; on the site,
  growth messaging is uncapped scale-up only (§6 #8, claim ledger #25,
  lint tests/lint-website-banned-phrases.test.ts).
- Purged invented vocabulary (design-pack storyboard relics that appear
  nowhere in the manuscript — never reintroduce in published copy):
  "machinery", "No magic", "watch it assemble". "qualified demand"
  LEFT the purge list 2026-08-10: the owner's cinematic brief
  (`design-source/nobull-revenue-engine-cinematic-v2/BRIEF.md` §7,
  Task #4295) re-adopted it verbatim in scenes 1–2, so it renders
  again (the #4837 engine story keeps it — overview + CaseGen ledes)
  and lint-website-banned-phrases no longer polices it. Code
  identifiers (nb-machinery), CSS classes, and asset filenames are
  exempt.
- The "avoid gears" rule above stands despite the book's single
  "well-fitted gears" line — fuel / injector / spark / flywheel stays
  the machine vocabulary.
- Mining is for voice and phrasing, never numbers: book statistics and
  promises (87%, 72%, 22%, "pay for itself", "change your life",
  client pseudonyms) stay out — the claim ledger governs.
