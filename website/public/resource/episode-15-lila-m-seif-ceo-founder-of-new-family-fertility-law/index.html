Episode #15 – Lila M. Seif | CEO & Founder of New Family Fertility Law

Today we interview Lila M. Seif!

Lila Seif is a uniquely specialized attorney who’s devoted her life and her career exclusively to helping families bring new life into the world through third party assisted reproduction technology. Over the last 6 years she’s represented over 500 intended parents both domestically and abroad. She’s a regular panelist and speaker at fertility law conferences and serves on the Board of the Legal Professionals Group of the American Society for Reproductive Medicine.
